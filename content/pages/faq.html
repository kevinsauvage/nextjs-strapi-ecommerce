Orders & shipping

Where do you ship?
We currently ship across Spain and France, with the rest of the EU rolling out soon. Delivery is free on orders over €80.

How long does delivery take?
Orders are prepared within 1–2 business days and typically arrive in 3–6 business days with tracked delivery.

How can I track my order?
Sign in and open My orders — every order has a detail page with live tracking links.

Sizing & care

How do I choose the right size?
See our pet care & sizing guide. If your pet is between two sizes, size up for beds and carriers.

How should I care for my products?
Most bed covers and cushions are removable and machine washable at 30°C. Check the care label on each product for exact instructions.

Returns & exchanges

What is the return window?
30 days from delivery, unused and in its original packaging. See our refund policy for the full terms.

Something arrived damaged?
Contact us through the contact page with your order number and a photo — we will make it right.

Account & payment

Do I need an account to order?
No, guest checkout works. An account makes tracking, reordering and address management faster.

Which payment methods do you accept?
All major cards and the express wallets shown at checkout. Payments are processed over an encrypted connection.
